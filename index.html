About Wheelhouse

Kwame opened Wheelhouse in 2019 after spending years fixing bikes for friends and neighbors out of his yard. The workshop now handles everything from kids' bikes to high-end road and mountain bikes. All repairs are done by Kwame personally or under his direct supervision.

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most often.

How long will my repair take?

Most basic repairs and tune-ups are done the same day, usually within an hour or two. More involved repairs like gear system overhauls or full rebuilds may need the bike overnight or for a couple of days. We will give you an honest estimate when you drop your bike off.

Do I need to book in advance?

You do not need an appointment for most repairs. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. If you have a large or urgent job, giving us a call ahead of time means we can make sure we have the right parts in stock before you arrive.

What brands and types of bike do you work on?

We work on all types of bicycles: road, mountain, hybrid, BMX, and kids' bikes. We are comfortable with most major brands. If you have something unusual, get in touch before you bring it in and we will let you know if it is something we can handle.

Will you call me before starting work?

Yes. After we inspect your bike, we will call or message you with a diagnosis and a price before we start any repair. Nothing happens without your approval. If the repair is straightforward and you have already agreed to a standard price, we will go ahead and notify you when it is done.
