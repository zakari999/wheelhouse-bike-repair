Ask us about your bike
Do I need to book ahead? +

Not for most repairs. Walk-ins are welcome during workshop hours. For major work or an urgent repair, calling ahead is a good idea.

How long does a repair take? +

Many simple jobs can be finished the same day, often within an hour or two. Full rebuilds and more involved repairs can take longer. We’ll give you a realistic estimate after inspecting the bike.

Will I know the price before work starts? +

Yes. We’ll explain what needs doing and confirm the price for your approval before starting the repair.

What kinds of bikes do you work on? +

We service road, mountain, hybrid, BMX, and children’s bikes. For unusual models or parts, check with us first.

What if my bike needs to stay overnight? +

For major repairs that require overnight storage, the workshop currently lists a GH₵ 10 per day storage fee. Please confirm the details when dropping off your bike.
